# Collège Anwal, Khénifra — public research dossier
Research cut-off: 26 November 2026  
School: الثانوية الإعدادية أنوال — Lycée collégial Anwal, Khénifra, Morocco

This dossier records material located through public web, image, video, news, government-document and map searches. French and Arabic name variants were checked with two search engines. It is a substantial public-source collection, not a claim to have recovered every photograph ever made or material hidden in private accounts.

## What was located

- 33 selected source records, including government documents, local reporting, school-event galleries and two directly indexed school videos.
- 63 distinct image-file references after merging one obvious size/format duplicate. Of these, 33 returned an image; 30 were linked by their articles but direct retrieval failed or redirected.
- 52 image references accompany Anwal-specific or Anwal-hosted events; 11 accompany shared regional/national events. The latter are context images, not automatically pictures of Anwal pupils.
- Two school-focused video records plus nine video embeds in a shared 2017 regional reception article. The nine embeds were not playable during research; their individual subject matter is unverified.
- No independently confirmed official school Facebook account, Instagram account, standalone official website, official logo, school telephone or school email was established.

Images include collages, article covers, resized files and shared-event pictures. A file reference is not proof of a unique original photograph. Image ownership and permission to republish were not established; the collection supplies source links rather than an image-download archive.

## Date and attribution checks

1. **Journalism workshop:** S26 is published 2 March 2026 but says Friday 27 February 2027 in its body. This is internally inconsistent. 27 February 2026 was a Friday, which suggests a typo, but the event year should be confirmed before publication.
2. **2026 academic awards:** S28 is published Friday 3 April 2026 but refers to “yesterday Wednesday.” Keep the school year / publication date; do not assign an exact ceremony day from that phrase.
3. **2019 cross-country:** S12 was published in January 2020 and describes a December 2019 championship. Use 2019 for the achievement.
4. **Summer-school reach:** The approximately 500 pupils in S30 belong to the provincial multi-centre programme, not an established Anwal-only enrolment.
5. **Medical-caravan reach:** The 2,600+ beneficiaries in S24 refer to the hospital caravan, not an Anwal pupil count.
6. **Robotics trophy mentioned in S31:** The 2022 Arab robotics award cited as background belongs to the 13 Novembre school in Elhri, not Anwal.
7. **Theatre prize in S30:** The award-winning play أمنا الأرض performed at the launch is not enough evidence to attribute the prize to Anwal.
8. **2017 reception:** The same article covers cross-country and a football national team. Its football results are not established Anwal achievements.
9. **Namesakes:** Results about Anwal schools in Tangier, Béni Mellal, Taounate/Tahar Souk, Settat, Taroudant and other cities were excluded unless the report explicitly tied the record to Khénifra.
